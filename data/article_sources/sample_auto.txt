Tesla Confirms Highly Anticipated 'Model Y Juniper' Refresh Details

Tesla has officially shared the first details regarding its upcoming Model Y refresh, internally codenamed "Juniper." The electric vehicle pioneer plans to introduce a series of exterior and interior upgrades designed to mirror changes made to the Model 3 "Highland" refresh.

The updated crossover will feature a redesigned front end with sleeker LED headlights, a new tail light strip extending across the rear liftgate, and newly styled aerodynamic wheels designed to increase efficiency. 

Inside the cabin, drivers can expect a cleaner dashboard layout with the deletion of steering column stalks in favor of steering wheel controls. An updated ambient lighting strip and a new 8-inch rear screen for backseat passengers will also be integrated.

Significantly, battery efficiency is expected to improve by 5% due to enhanced aerodynamics, pushing the Long Range model's range close to 330 miles on a single charge. 

Production of the Model Y Juniper is scheduled to begin at Gigafactory Shanghai and Gigafactory Berlin late this year, with a North American rollout anticipated in early 2027.
