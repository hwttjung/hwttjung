Original Title: NASA Webb Telescope Uncovers Warm Water Vapor in Planetary Cradle
Original Summary: New observations from NASA's James Webb Space Telescope have revealed abundant warm water vapor located in the inner terrestrial planet-forming zone of young star system PDS 70. This remarkable finding indicates that essential volatile ingredients for life and oceans can withstand intense juvenile stellar radiation and remain present during terrestrial planet assembly.
